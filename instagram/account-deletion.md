# Deleting your Instagram account

> Draft. Steps follow Meta's Accounts Center as of 2026; labels may change.

## 1. Export your data first

Accounts Center → **Your information and permissions** → **Download your
information**. Choose *All available information*, format *JSON* (machine
readable) or *HTML* (easy to browse). The export can take up to a few days.

## 2. Optional: clean activity before deleting

Your comments on other people's posts disappear when the account is deleted,
but they can be cached or quoted before that. If it matters, run
[activity-cleaner](activity-cleaner/) first.

## 3. Delete the account

Accounts Center → **Personal details** → **Account ownership and control** →
**Deactivation or deletion** → **Delete account**.

- Meta keeps the account recoverable for **30 days**. Do not log in during that
  time, or the deletion is cancelled.
- Full removal from Meta's systems can take up to **90 days**.

## 4. After deletion

- Revoke the Instagram login from any third-party apps you connected.
- Check search engines for cached profile pages and request removal if needed.
