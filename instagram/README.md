# Instagram

| Tool | Use it when |
|------|-------------|
| [activity-cleaner](activity-cleaner/) | You keep the account but want your likes, comments, reposts, and story replies gone |
| [account-deletion.md](account-deletion.md) | You want the account and its data gone for good |

Removing activity does not erase copies others made (screenshots, quotes,
caches). Deleting the account is the only complete option on Instagram's side.
